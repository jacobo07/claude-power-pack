# Paid UGC machine (Superwall podcast, Dillian / Halo AI)

Published 2026-03-29. AI photo-editor app reported at $300k MRR in 45 days via paid user-generated
content (UGC). Off-topic for PP; kept as a **decision guide for app growth**. Sponsored podcast; all
numbers self-reported.

## Decision guide

| decision | what the source suggests |
|---|---|
| How to model growth? | Revenue = views × conversion. Views = creators × videos/creator × posts/video (platforms) × views/post. A given format's conversion stays roughly constant, so each factor can be improved on its own. |
| When to spend big? | Once one video showed a ~6:1 return on ad spend, he took a $100k loan. Scaled at break-even first; now ~50% margin. |
| Where to find creators? | Post a job in UGC Facebook groups and Reddit; ~500-600 applicants. Screen for past UGC experience and US-based; 5-video paid trial ($20/video). |
| Trial pass criteria? | Consistency, proactive communication, visible hook → build-up → payoff editing. Not raw views (virality follows a power law). |
| How many formats? | One fixed format: absurd AI image hook → app demo with a big logo on the loading screen → text-message prank story with a twist. "One format, 1.2 billion views." |
| How to pay creators? | $20 base + view milestones (20k/100k/500k/1M). 29 of 30 creators picked it over a slightly better-paying CPM deal. Simpler to operate; one bonus per video, not per platform. |
| How to scale hits? | Send every viral video to all creators to recreate the same day. |
| Paywall? | Skippable paywall during onboarding + a hard paywall at the first real action, together converting better than either alone; weekly plan ($8.99). |
| Which niches last? | Health, wealth, relationships retain; entertainment goes viral but churns. |
