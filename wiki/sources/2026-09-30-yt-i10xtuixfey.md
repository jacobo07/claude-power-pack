# Creator-led app distribution (Superwall podcast, Casper / Payout)

Published 2026-03-15. Interview with a finance creator who co-owns a class-action-claims app (Payout,
reported $217k in 5 months). Off-topic for PP; kept as a **decision guide for app growth**.
Sponsored podcast (Superwall, SpyTok ad); all numbers are self-reported.

## Decision guide

| decision | what the source suggests |
|---|---|
| Which app idea? | Mine a creator's past **sponsored** videos: the ones that performed organically are pre-validated ideas. Organic vs boosted: ~2M views with ~2k likes = boosted; ~50k likes = organic. |
| How to recruit a creator? | Bring a built app based on their best-performing ad, offer equity (he cites 50%), ask only for repeats of that format. |
| Which content format? | Find one format that converts, repeat it with small variations (numbers, backgrounds) until it dries up, then find the next. He ran two formats in total. |
| How to pitch in-video? | Never say the app's name ("go to this app"); focus on what the viewer gets ("the bag"), not the story; soft CTA in a pinned comment. |
| Which category? | "Spend X, get more than X back" offers sell easily; niches with steady demand (side hustles) beat hit-or-miss ones. |
| Launch timing? | Several creators posting the same day pushed the app up the store charts ($9.7k that day). |
| Equity split? | Weighted toward whoever has business judgement; his third app gave 15% to the dev side. |

## Caveats

Survivor story, one creator, one niche (US finance). Short-form videos keep producing downloads for
months — the only claim backed by a shown chart.
