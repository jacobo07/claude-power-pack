  (Phase 3).
- ASC-04 CCF integrity defects proven fixed, or fixed RED -> GREEN with a regression (Phase 4).
- ASC-05 A constitutive baseline promoted through the existing owner, with a fresh-agent inheritance proof: the incomplete
  variant is rejected and the correct one passes (Phase 5).
