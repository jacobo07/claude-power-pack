- FC-09 Policy changes are benchmarked by historical replay (champion/challenger) with mutants that go red.
- FC-10 No GSD Core modification; no second lifecycle, orchestrator or mission state machine.
- FC-11 Reports separate summed mission-hours, wall-clock and critical-path wall-clock.
