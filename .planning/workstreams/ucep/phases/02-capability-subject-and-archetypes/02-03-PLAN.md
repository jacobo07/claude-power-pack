Cause precedence for a trait with no positive (PRESENT or WEAK) evidence, applied by one function `_entitle(trait, found, walk)`:
1. the trait has no structural detector at all (bulk, destructive) -> UNJUDGED `no-structural-detector`
2. the walk stopped at `cap` -> UNJUDGED `truncated`
3. the walk stopped at `budget_s` -> UNJUDGED `budget-exhausted`
4. at least one directory could not be listed -> UNJUDGED `unreadable-subtree`
5. the trait has a dependency-class detector and no manifest was parsed -> UNJUDGED `no-manifest-ecosystem`
6. otherwise ABSENT, fact_state OBSERVED, reason naming the ecosystems seen.
PRESENT if any STRONG evidence exists, else WEAK if any WEAK evidence exists, regardless of the walk outcome (a positive found before a cut stays valid).
