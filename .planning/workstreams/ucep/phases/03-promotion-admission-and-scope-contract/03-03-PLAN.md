<objective>
Make admission a property of the chain: every entry added at a generation outside the pinned identity set must carry a valid record, so a raw `write_generation` can no longer bypass `promote`. Before switching that on, give synthetic test chains an explicit, refusable way to stand in for pre-cutover generations (`legacy=`), and migrate the four Phase 1 suites that build such chains, so their gates keep measuring what they measured.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criterion 3 (`verify_chain` refuses any entry added at the cutover or later without a record) and the unadmitted-raw-write half of criterion 5 (UCEP-03); audit G5.
Output: `legacy=` on `verify_chain` and `donegate.judge`, the UNADMITTED regression, the restated WR-01 residual, six new gates, four migrated suites with unchanged counts.

How the existing suites stay green (03-CONTEXT A3 left the choice to the planner): each synthetic chain whose B0 is a raw fixture passes `legacy=(0,)` explicitly; chains that must be admitted for real (the CLI paths, which run with `root=None`) are built through `promote` with evidenced entries. `legacy=` is refused outright for every spelling of the real tree (compared after `realpath`, `normcase` and separator stripping) and for any path ending `vault/tower/baselines`, so it cannot reach the real tree. Copies of the real `web_surface/B0.json` used by `test_ucep_baseline_integrity` are grandfathered by identity (A1) and need nothing. Why not records in every fixture: the donegate suites deliberately hold prose and empty checks to prove UNJUDGED, and such an entry can never be admitted, so only a declared legacy generation can host it.

Decision trace (03-CONTEXT IDs): A2 cutover = every generation outside the pinned set; A3 no default switches the requirement off for root=None, every Phase 1 suite keeps or grows its count; B4 UNADMITTED is a chain regression; D1 `revert`/`reanchor` need no admission; Claude's Discretion "every refusal reason is a named constant" (the UNADMITTED reason gets a token); E1 RED first (the raw-add gates are the named existing-behaviour case: raw adds pass today); E2 refusal-control pairs; specifics (WR-01 docstring restated precisely, not implied closed).
This plan implements A2, A3, B4 (UNADMITTED), D1 (proof), E1, E2 and the WR-01 specifics item.
</objective>
