Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 3 success criteria 1 and 2 and the `glob:**` / narrow-scope halves of criterion 5 (UCEP-03); audit G6 (a self-declared narrow scope auto-admitted).
Output: the extended `admission.py`, the PENDING_OWNER refusal text in `ratchet.promote`, ten new gates.

Decision trace (03-CONTEXT IDs): B2 record fields (check, evidence refs, negative applicability, counterfactual); C1 scope from location, widened by applicability and by counterfactual `constitutive`; C2 declared narrower refused, declared wider raises the bar; C3 family/archetype -> AUTO_ADMITTED, cross-family/universal/constitutive -> PENDING_OWNER; C4 PENDING_OWNER is not written, `promote` names the missing Owner decision; C5 counterfactual `instance` refused; C6 refused whatever the scope (match-everything check, missing evidence ref or negative applicability, empty `manual:`); C7 every failing reason; E1 RED first; E2 each refusal paired with an admitted control.
This plan implements B2 (check and claim fields), C1 (applicability, constitutive), C2, C3, C4, C5, C6 (check and claim part), C7 (complete), E1, E2.
