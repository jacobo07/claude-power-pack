## Registry design
~40 questions in the tool: `{id, question, gate (V-id), evidence (file under the workstream or a log), planes_needed: PLATFORM|PORTABILITY, row}`. Status per question, computed not stored: `CLOSED` when the evidence file exists, holds a `PASS <gate>` line, and the plane recorded in the evidence covers the claim; `OPEN` when no evidence; `UNKNOWN` when the evidence is unreadable or has the gate neither PASS nor FAIL; `SINGLE_PLANE` when a PORTABILITY claim has one plane. Exit 1 while any question is not CLOSED. Plane evidence is read from a `plane:` marker line `plane=<id> modules_tree=<oid>` in the evidence file; PORTABILITY needs two distinct ids with one tree (the same predicate as `trust.portability_ok`).
Final PASS (not this wave) additionally needs liveness 0, DENOMINATOR dispositioned, `family_baseline verify`, fresh-agent report.
