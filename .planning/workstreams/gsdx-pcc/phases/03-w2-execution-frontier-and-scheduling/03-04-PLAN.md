# 03-04: write authority and sweep dispatch of N independent gate epochs

Goal: the one-open-epoch rule is relaxed only at the authority (`epoch.begin`), only for stored-`unit` independent epochs, and the sweep dispatches up to `bound` of them.

## Tasks (RED first)
1. **RED** (second RED-list item, "unsafe parallel writes"): gates driven against current code. V-FRONTIER-UNSAFE-WRITES builds two units with overlapping `writes` and asserts the SECOND `begin` raises `EpochError`; it passes against legacy (still refuses) and must STILL pass after GREEN, so the RED proof is the mutation drill in 03-06 (mutant makes `begin` admit without checking independence; the gate must kill it). V-FRONTIER-BEGIN-ADMITS and V-FRONTIER-SWEEP-N FAIL now. Log `03-04-red.log`.
2. **GREEN** `epoch.py`: `begin(..., unit: dict | None = None)` (new trailing keyword, default `None`). The still-open refusal stays exactly as-is unless ALL hold: `unit` given, `frontier.frontier_enabled()`, every open epoch has a stored `spec["unit"]`, and `proof.independent(unit, other)` for every pair. Then admit; store `unit` in the epoch `spec` (frontier path only, so legacy specs and the corpus are byte-identical). Lazy import to avoid a cycle.
3. **GREEN** `sweep.py` `sweep_goal`: after the existing dispatch branch, while the last decision was a gate NEXT_EPOCH with `spec["unit"]`, re-project (`gc.project`), rebuild the Context, call `rc.decide` again, at most `bound-1` more times; stop on any other decision or on `LostRace`/`EpochError` (recorded in `acted`, never swallowed silently). Dry-run reports "would run" for each. Pass `unit=d.spec.get("unit")` to `ep.begin`. With bound 1 the loop body never runs.

## Gates
- V-FRONTIER-BEGIN-REFUSES: no unit, or a switch-off, or an open epoch lacking a stored unit, or an overlapping/undeclared pair: second begin raises, fence unchanged.
- V-FRONTIER-UNSAFE-WRITES (RED 2): overlapping `writes`, read-after-write, and None sets all refused at the authority regardless of what the caller (the reconciler) said.
- V-FRONTIER-BEGIN-ADMITS (control): disjoint declared units admitted; fences strictly increasing 1,2,3.
- V-FRONTIER-SWEEP-N: real `sweep_goal` on a temp git repo with three independent trivial gates (`python -c "pass"`) and posture WALL_CLOCK dispatches 3 epochs in one pass; ECONOMY dispatches 1.
- V-FRONTIER-SWEEP-BOUND: bound never exceeded, including `max_parallel: 2` under WALL_CLOCK.
- V-FRONTIER-LOSTRACE: two dispatchers from one stale state: exactly one begin wins, the other gets LostRace/refusal; no duplicate fence.
- V-FRONTIER-FENCE: harvesting each of N concurrent epochs ingests only with its own fence/run_token; a receipt with a sibling's fence is refused (`epoch.py:348-356`).
