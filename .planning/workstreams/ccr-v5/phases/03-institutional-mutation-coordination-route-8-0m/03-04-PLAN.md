  Resume exactly once: the transition OPEN to RESUMING is written (fsync, replace) before spawn_fn is called, and the pid is recorded by a second write. A RESUMING row with no pid is never spawned again: it is ESCALATED with reason RESUME_INTENT_WITHOUT_PROCESS (the process may or may not have started, so the wake cannot know whether a landing happened). A second wake_pass call, from any process, sees RESUMING and only observes. Concurrent passes are serialized by the _Lock on the waits directory held across read-decide-write of one row.
  RESUMING observation order: (1) read the receipt (a parse error is treated as not yet complete while the process may still be writing); (2) a parseable receipt goes through receipt_to_transition; (3) no usable receipt and alive_fn (default lr._pid_alive on the recorded pid) True: action await, except that a row older than the landing bound (3600 s, a named constant, longer than the 1800 s per-test timeout of run_tests) is ESCALATED with reason LANDING_OVERRUN and the process is left running (the wake never signals a process); (4) alive_fn False and no usable receipt: ESCALATED with reason LANDING_DIED_NO_RECEIPT when the receipt is absent and RECEIPT_UNREADABLE when it exists but does not parse, never retried because a partial landing is possible; (5) alive_fn None is treated like True until the overrun bound. A row ESCALATED for a dead or overrun landing is reported with an `error` field so the supervise exit code and the sweep log carry it; so is every ROLLBACK_FAILED transition. Other escalations are reported as action wait_escalated without `error`.
  RETRY transitions: runtime.attempts increases by one, runtime.last_refusal records status and reason, state returns to OPEN, and the next resume happens at a later pass, never the same pass; the third RETRY is ESCALATED with reason RETRIES_EXHAUSTED.
  Follow-up row: on DONE_CARVED_OUT the pass opens `<wait_id>.carry` through wait_open (resources are the carved paths, expected events COMMIT, CLEARED and WRITER_DEAD, a fresh timeout of the original timeout length, the same resume action and validation, and an absent union). If wait_open reports NO_CONFLICT the carried paths are already clear and the pass records follow_up NOT_NEEDED on the finished row; if it fails any other way the finished row records follow_up FAILED with the reason and the pass reports an `error` row, because a carved change with no follow-up is exactly the silent loss the carve-out design forbids.
