Your meeting ended. Your to-do list is already written.

Upload a recording and Minutehand returns the decisions and action items from it, each with an owner and a due date, usually before you are back at your desk.

Compare plans

Less note-taking, fewer dropped tasks

Owners, not just bullet points

Every action item names who said they would do it, taken from the conversation itself, so nobody has to guess whose job it is.

Dates people actually said

"By Friday" becomes a real date. When nobody committed to one, the item is marked as open instead of getting an invented deadline.

Jump back to the moment

Each item links to the timestamp in the recording where it was agreed, so you can check the exact wording in seconds.
