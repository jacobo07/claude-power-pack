Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This page describes what happens to your data when you visit this website. Minutehand has not launched yet, so this site is a static preview: it has no accounts, no forms and no uploads.

What we collect

Nothing directly. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

Server logs

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages may record standard request data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. These logs are used only to keep the site running and secure.

When the product launches

Processing meeting recordings involves personal data. Before any signup or upload is offered, this page will be updated to explain what is stored, where, for how long, and how to delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because this preview collects none beyond routine server logs, there is currently nothing linked to you to retrieve.
