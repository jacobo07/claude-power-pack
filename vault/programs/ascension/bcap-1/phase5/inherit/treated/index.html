Harbour Tide Letter

Once a month, the coming month's high and low tides for the harbour, in one short email.

Sign up by email

The button opens your mail app with a short message ready to send. We add you by hand, so your first letter arrives at the start of the next month.

What you get

  • High and low water times and heights for every day of the month.
  • One email at the start of each month. Nothing else.
  • To stop, reply with the word "stop".

Before you set out

The letter is a planning aid. Weather and pressure can shift the tide, so check the harbour office's notices on the day.
