## Obligation (scope)
A lease ends in a worker that stopped. Today nobody decides, atomically and once, what happens next. Build:
1. Terminal reconciliation: given a sid and evidence, classify TERMINAL_PROVEN (process-proven dead: the caller passes a
   `dead: bool` from a process probe, never inferred from absent files; measured spend known) vs UNKNOWN_RESERVED (not proven:
   the hold stays reserved, with a TTL; after the TTL a sweep may convert it only by measured value). Unknown is never free.
   Terminal => ledger.settle_stopped at the measured value. Put it in the ledger or a sibling module, your call, reason in the receipt.
2. NEW tools/goal_succession.py: one entry `succeed(goal, succession_id, prev_lease, cap, reserves, workspace, expect_gen, new_sid)`:
   settle the dead holder (step 1), lease_close(prev) then lease_open(succession_id, CAS on prev_lease), then registry.claim(gen+1)
   for the successor. Journalled per succession id; calling it twice with one id changes nothing after the first call (every
   sub-step is itself idempotent, and a crash between sub-steps resumes on the next call). Refusals (AUTHORITY_REQUIRED,
   CAS lost) are returned, never raised, and never leave a half-succession: either the new holder is claimed or the old one still holds.
3. Call site: the chain tick on `stopped_without_done` and on budget halts. Locate with ONE Grep for `stopped_without_done` over
   tools/ and vault/programs/dgl/ (anchors: vault/programs/dgl/dgl_chain_tick.ps1, unit_done.py, chain.json; the live chain driver is
   tools/gsdx_chain.py, do not edit it; add a copy-free hook point only if the driver has one, else a small tools/goal_chain.py
   that the tick calls, and say so). Wiring beyond the tick is W4.
