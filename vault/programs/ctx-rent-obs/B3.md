## UNATTRIBUTED
**54,797 tokens per session (49.7% of the mean 110,190 floor)**, never folded into a named row. Per the census it is
tool schemas, framing and estimate error. In the two sessions inspected in full, the split is: host prefix 33,954 and 33,931
(call-1 cache_read shared across sessions) and 21-25K session-specific unattributed; at the history-calibrated 2.1-2.3
chars/token the session-specific residual shrinks by 8-11K, so a share of it is estimator error, not content. W8
cannot separate tool-schema bytes from that error without a probe, and W8 runs no probes.
