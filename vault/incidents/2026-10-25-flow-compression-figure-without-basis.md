# Incident: figures printed without their basis (2026-10-25, phase 7)

**Class:** FIGURE WITHOUT BASIS -> A NUMBER THAT READS AS MEASURED WHILE IT IS CARRIED, INFERRED OR ASSUMED.
Four figures in the S7 replay work were quoted without the fact that made them true or false: a carried-
unchanged idle figure that reads as a gain, a summary figure computed over a subset, a headline total that
is mostly unobserved time, and a STATE root cause stated without being isolated.

## Symptom

- Carried-unchanged idle figure: the idle-gap seam answers the same under either arm, so the challenger idle
  figure for a gap no seam recomputed is the champion figure. Printed bare, it reads as a gain. It is now
  labelled carried unchanged (STATE.md line 73).
- The 07-01 summary printed 7 avoidable idle gaps at 0.728 h and 23 unmeasured. The committed report prints
  66 idle gaps: 22 avoidable, 36 legitimate, 8 with incomplete inputs, avoidable 1.353 h before and after,
  0.651 h unmeasured. The 07-01 summary counted only the 7 gaps with a start-ack-overdue timer (STATE.md
  line 83; 07-VERIFICATION W3, which also read the report itself).
- STATE.md gave a root cause for 215 incidents under the test against 266 under the CLI (import order). The
  verifier isolated a different cause: the pin helper in the test builds its pin over a copy with no
  mission records, so the 51-incident S5 slice is empty. Corrected in c1d17703.
- The summed mission-hours headline of 27,483.911 h is about 94 percent unobserved time: 92 of 148 missions
  are open at the pin and contribute 26,706 h, counted as running to the end of the pin (07-VERIFICATION W1;
  bounded at their last row the total is about 1,650 h; 27,484 h over 715 h of wall clock implies 38x
  concurrency, which did not happen).

## Root cause

A figure was copied between documents (plan, summary, STATE, report) without its denominator and its
basis. The summary reported the subset it had built so far; the STATE line reported a cause that had not
been isolated; the headline total summed open intervals the data does not bound. Nothing in the report
writer forced a number to carry the word measured, inferred, carried or assumed until the S7 tagged-report
writer did.

## Why the existing rule did not prevent it

"State measured figures with their instrument" was practice, not mechanism. Summaries are prose, so no
check compared them with the report that is regenerated from the pin.

## Earliest discoverable point

The first time a second document quoted a figure from the first. Regenerating the report from the pin and
diffing the numbers against the summary, at the plan close, would have shown 7 against 22 on 2026-10-25.

## Repair

The report writer refuses an untagged number and an empty tag (V-S7-REPORT-TAGGED); carried-unchanged and
inferred figures are labelled (V-S7-IDLE-BEFORE-AFTER); unmeasured is kept apart from zero
(V-S7-TIME-UNMEASURED-NOT-ZERO, V-S7-REPORT-UNMEASURED-APART); the three time numbers are never merged
(V-S7-TIME-NEVER-CONFLATED). The two false statements were corrected in c1d17703. The report is the figure
of record.

## Pattern

figure quoted in a second document is regenerated from its source, not copied.
