# Part XXVI — Integration Map and Institutional Writeback

## 1. Purpose

This Part closes the family, and it does so under the family's own rules. Part XX established that
closure is an accounting act with five available verdicts, that the producer closes the accounting
while a constituency accepts, and that a closure omitting what remains is a completeness claim
wearing closure's name. Part XXIV established that a family assessing itself with a pass/fail
predicate would be committing its own founding error at the last step.

So this Part resolves CLAE against every canonical owner it touches, states what it consumes and
produces, reports four **levels** rather than a compliance figure, declares what it owes, and issues
a closure verdict that is not *complete*.

## 2. The integration map

Every owner named in the charter's non-scope table, plus those identified during construction. The
column that matters is the third: what CLAE does **not** touch.

The pattern is deliberate and worth stating: **CLAE adds fields, criteria and vocabulary to existing
surfaces, and replaces none of them.** Every proposal in the family is additive. That was the
constraint Phase 0 imposed when it measured that most of what the corpus proposed was already owned
here, and the map is the evidence that the constraint held.

## 3. Consumes, produces, registers

**Consumes:** the reachability audit's discovery discipline · the artifact done gate's contracts ·
the design scorer as the proven graded instance · the recurring-error log's identity scheme · the
findings transport · the hard-rule archive's provenance model.

**Produces:** the residual ledger with identity, mode and coverage · floor derivations with threshold
placement · oracle boundary declarations · Phase Zero verdicts with measurement-debt registers ·
deviation records with measured loss · the four-point gate set with label propagation · durable
probes from incidents.

**Registers into:** the rule archive (118 process rules, labelled deductive) · the trap registry (99
entries) · the eval set · the compendium integration spine.

## 4. What CLAE deliberately did not build

Recorded because a family's restraint is as much a result as its output, and because Phase 0's
central finding was that the corpus proposed a great deal this stack already had.

Not built: a rule placement compiler; a second semantic graph; a succession mechanism; a
just-in-time loader; an epistemic ladder; a side-effect detector; an egress gateway; a dashboard;
a decompiler; a resource-residency runtime. Each has a canonical owner, was verified during the
Phase 0 audit, and is referenced rather than duplicated.

## 5. The family's own closure

Applying Part XX, in its own vocabulary.

**Verdict: complete with residual.**

Not *complete*. Twenty-six Parts are sealed, each above the depth floor, each gated. The intent —
supply this stack with the discipline of measured distance to an external bar — is served at the
level of doctrine. A measured gap remains, and it is stated below rather than omitted.

**What was done.** Twenty-six Parts, roughly 500,000 bytes, one per commit, pathspec-scoped, each
gated on contamination, code fences, coherence and a depth floor before its index row was flipped.
Two gate failures occurred and both correctly aborted before committing.

**What remains, by dimension:**

- **Registry extracts.** The index names twelve companion artifacts. The registries themselves exist
  as Parts XXII through XXV — that is where the schemas, consolidations and measured counts live. The
  separate files would be extracts for retrieval convenience, and they are not produced. Open.
- **Rule overlap.** 118 process rules are distinct by name and unmeasured for semantic overlap. Part
  XXIII §16 states the expectation that the effective count is materially lower. Open.
- **Gate arity.** This build's gate cannot report could-not-observe. Part XXIV §9 tested and confirmed
  its detection capability; the arity gap stands. Open, and named in three Parts.
- **Gate registration.** The gate script is unregistered after twenty-six uses — Part XIV §8's third
  state that is supposed not to exist. Open.
- **Companion families.** CRPF and IGEF, and extension passes E1 through E5, are unbuilt. Open, and
  the largest remaining item by volume.

**What could not be measured:**

- Whether the doctrine works in practice. Every claim here is derived from the corpus, the Phase 0
  audit, and reasoning. None has been applied to a live correction loop and measured. This is the
  family's principal undefined dimension and no Part conceals it.
- Whether the extraction ladder, the four impact factors and the five closure verdicts are complete
  sets. Each Part's open questions record the doubt.
- Lineage L5's position in this stack, which would require reading the intent records of past work.

**Deviations:** two. The generated spec skeleton was left unfilled and reported rather than
completed, on the constraint that its title was derived from a shell command rather than the task.
The global configuration trim was reported rather than applied, on the constraint that the tool's
only strategy was exhausted and the remaining candidates sat inside sections marked critical. Both
were recorded at the time. Neither loss was measured, which is precisely the failure Part XVIII §10
names — this family's own escape routes carry no loss accounting either.

**Judgments self-answered versus oracle-answered:** almost all self-answered. One oracle question was
routed — the architecture choice at the Phase 0 stop — and Part XVII §10 records that it was taken as
a **verdict** where a criterion was available, so the two unbuilt families will need to ask again.
Every quality judgment about this family's own content was made by its author.

**Acceptance line: empty.** Per Part XX §6, the producer closes the accounting and a constituency
accepts. This closure is accounting-only.

## 6. Four benchmarks, reported as levels

Per Part XXIV §7. None of these has a passing value.

**Lineage position.** L6 instrument decay: confirmed, links one through four in place. L1 blindness:
partial. L4 myopia: likely. L3 metric theater: partial. L2 bar inflation: absent, because the
precondition is absent. L5 replanning amnesia: unassessed.

**Enforcement layer distribution.** A small handful reachable at layer one; roughly nineteen checks at
layer two if the four gate points are wired; the majority at layer five. Part XXIII §5 states the
consequence without softening: a doctrine of 118 rules at layer five has approximately the force of a
doctrine of zero rules, and the operating subset is the ten high-recognition traps plus the four gate
points.

**Derivation ratio.** A minority of rules trace to observations made during this build — the
two-valued instruments, the absent tracing instrument, the unregistered gate, the
reserved-decisions-without-declared-judgments gap, the handoff block's three verdicts. The majority
are inferences from those. The family is largely hypothesis and says so.

**Measurement debt.** Named above in §5. The dimension that matters most is the first: no claim here
has been tested against a live loop.

## 7. Institutional writeback — where the findings go

Each finding, its owner, and the change it implies. These are the actionable residue of the build,
independent of whether any of the doctrine is adopted.

1. **Every instrument in the stack is two-valued.** Widening output to include could-not-observe is a
   type change at each boundary and retires an entire failure class — the highest-leverage item found.
2. **No tracing or profiling instrument exists**, capping extraction at L3 outside resource
   dimensions and making corrections reasoned rather than measured.
3. **No surface records a noise floor**, so no residual can distinguish a small gap from an
   unresolvable one.
4. **No surface has residual identity**; the recurring-error log's scheme is the place to start.
5. **Reserved decisions exist; a declared judgment boundary does not.** The first governs what the
   system may do, the second what it may conclude, and having one is no evidence of the other.
6. **The hard-rule archive has better provenance than this family and lacks four fields** — layer,
   root, retirement, eval. Adding them would reveal how much of a 156-entry corpus operates.
7. **Incidents convert to rules and almost never to probes.** The stack's failure knowledge is
   advisory rather than detective; the secret firewall is the counterexample and the model.
8. **Escape routes carry no loss accounting.** The exception clauses are well-formed and record no
   trade, so deviation density and lapsed constraints are both invisible.
9. **No labelling gate exists.** Every gate blocks, so a qualified pass has no representation.
10. **The handoff block is the strongest closure instrument** and needs two small additions: a
    dimensioned debt field and an unmeasured-scope line.
11. **Numeric quality thresholds are undefended** while the parallel tool-call caps are a textbook
    derived floor; both sit in one document with equal authority.
12. **Two hand-curated indexes are stale** — the skill catalogue and the vault index — and need
    discovery producers rather than manual refreshes, which is the same defect the reachability audit
    was built to fix.

## 8. What the next families inherit

CRPF and IGEF inherit this family's vocabulary (Part III), its reference discipline (Parts IV and V),
its instrument contract (Part XIII) and its closure semantics (Part XX). They should not redefine any
of those; Part III §13's one-meaning rule is family-wide.

They also inherit an obligation this family discharged imperfectly: **ask for the criterion.** The
architecture decision that scoped all three families was taken as a verdict, so the criterion behind
it must be obtained before the next family's scope is set, or the same question will be purchased a
third time.

And they inherit the correction to this family's own charter, recorded in Part II §6 and carried in
the resumption file: the design review scorer is a stage-two graded criterion instrument, not a
stage-three referenced distance. That phrasing is not to be restored anywhere.

## 9. The family's own compliance with itself

Stated because a doctrine exempting its own artifact would be Part I at the last step.

**Followed:** joint publication — every commit carries its gate results. Discovery over declaration —
the trap, rule and gate counts were measured from the sealed text, not recalled. Negative control —
Part XXIV tested this build's own gate rather than asserting it works. Halts recorded as outcomes —
three declared halts, none recorded as failure. Five verdicts — this closure is *complete with
residual*, not *complete*.

**Not followed:** the evidence contract was distributed rather than written as one artifact (Part XIX
§11). The two deviations carry no measured loss (§5). The instrument gating twenty-six Parts is
unregistered and two-valued. Registry extracts are unproduced.

That list is the honest form of a completion report, and it is longer than a compliant one would be.
A family whose closing act was a clean self-assessment would have demonstrated nothing except that it
could grade itself.

## 9a. The open questions that matter

The Parts record roughly seventy open questions. Five of them determine whether the rest of the
family is usable, and they are consolidated here so that nobody has to read twenty-six Parts to find
the load-bearing doubts.

**Does discovery-manufactured externality generalize?** Part IV §7 argued that a set discovered from
what exists cannot be edited by the assessing party, which supplies externality where no exemplar is
available. It is demonstrated for a set-membership question and argued for everything else. If it
does not generalize to graded dimensions, the family applies only where external instances already
exist, which is a small fraction of engineering work. This is the single most load-bearing open item.

**Can effect-level location be computed?** Part IX §2 anchors residual identity to it, and Part VI §6
requires deltas to be extracted at it. If it needs a judgment per observation rather than a
computation, then identity becomes expensive enough to threaten the ledger, and the ledger is what
every trend, starvation check and oscillation detector depends on.

**Is the seeing-blocker classification mechanical?** Part XIV §2 makes it the trigger for stopping
production, and Part XIV §3 shows that judgment at exactly that moment is systematically biased. A
trigger requiring unbiased judgment at the point where judgment is biased may not fire when it is
most needed.

**Is L5 detectable before its point of no return?** Part XXI §16 records that replanning amnesia is
the only lineage with an unrecoverable terminal, that its progression is invisible, and that
detecting it means noticing an absence — which Part II §P8 establishes is what instruments do worst.

**How much of the 118 is genuinely distinct?** Part XXIII §16 expects the effective rule count to be
materially lower than the named count. Until the clustering probe runs, the family's stated size is
an upper bound rather than a measurement.

## 9b. Adopting a subset

The family is large and its layer-five majority means adopting all of it changes nothing. Four
interventions carry most of the value and can be taken independently, in this order.

**First, widen instrument output.** Add could-not-observe as a third value at each instrument
boundary. This is a type change, it is layer one, it retires an entire class of failure that Parts
II, IX, X, XII, XIII and XXV each fought procedurally, and it requires adopting none of the rest of
the doctrine.

**Second, retain the pre-threshold value.** Where a gate computes a magnitude and applies a
threshold, record the magnitude. Part II §2's retention thesis costs a field, not an instrument, and
it converts a large number of existing binary gates into graded ones without changing what they check.

**Third, publish the undefined set.** Every quality summary states the dimensions it could not
measure. Part IX §5's measurement debt is invisible by default and is the form the trap takes in a
system that has otherwise done everything right.

**Fourth, add the second closure verdict.** *Complete with residual* is the state most work is
actually in, and systems lacking the phrase report it as *complete*. One enumerated value.

None of these requires a reference, a ledger, a correction cycle or an evidence contract. Each is
independently useful, each is cheap, and together they address the four expressiveness findings that
turned out to be this family's most portable result.

## 10. Closing

The corpus that began this build proposed ten families of mechanisms and stated, in every round, that
it could not inspect this workspace. Phase 0 measured the overlap and found most of the proposals
already owned here at equal or greater maturity. What survived was ten verified gaps, and this family
addressed five of them.

Its thesis is one sentence: **every quality gate in this stack answers a binary question against a
criterion the stack itself authored, and after a pass, none of them reports what remains.** Twenty-six
Parts elaborate that into a reference object, a residual, an instrument contract, a floor derivation,
an oracle boundary and a closure vocabulary — and the family's most useful outputs turned out to be
four observations about expressiveness rather than any of its mechanisms.

Closure has two verdicts where five states exist. Instruments have two outputs where three are
needed. Rules have one force where five layers exist. Gates have two verdicts where a qualified pass
has no representation. **In every case the true state was reported as its nearest available
neighbour, and the neighbour was always the more favourable one.** That is not dishonesty and it
cannot be fixed by discipline. It is a vocabulary limit, and the remedy is to widen what can be said.

The family closes accounting-only, complete with residual, with its acceptance line empty.
