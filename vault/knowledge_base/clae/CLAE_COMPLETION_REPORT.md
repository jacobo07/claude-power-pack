The trap count is the control case: Part XXII asserted 100 occurrences, 99 distinct, one multi-Part
name, and the extraction reproduced all three exactly. A corpus whose counts are *sometimes* right
is the condition that makes measuring them worthwhile.

Two of the five sit in the ratifying scope document rather than in the corpus, and both overstate.
That is the sharper finding: **a scope description authored outside a corpus carries hypotheses
about it, not measurements of it** — and the correct response is to deliver what exists, record the
difference, and amend nothing that is sealed.

### 4.1 A claim that does not hold as written

Part XXV §4 states: *"The consolidation loses nothing — every seeded gate survives as a check
within a point."* Two seeded gates appear in no gate point by name — **Residual Ledger (Part IX)**
and **Oracle Routing (Part XVII)**. Each has an adjacent-Part neighbour of similar subject
(residual visibility, oracle boundary), so a silent merge is the likely explanation, but the text
does not say so.

`CLAE_PRODUCTION_GATES.md` §4 records both rows unresolved rather than deciding them. A reader
implementing the four points from Part XXV alone would not know two seeded gates had no home.

## 8. The family's own compliance with itself

Stated because a doctrine exempting its own artifact would be Part I at the last step.

**Followed.** Discovery over declaration — every count in §2 was measured, and §4 is what that
produced. Joint publication — each commit carries its gate results. Five verdicts — this closure is
*complete with residual*, not *complete*. Halting is not failing — the extension began with a
refusal to proceed without ratification, and that refusal is recorded as an outcome, not a defect.
Nothing sealed was amended to make a number agree.

**Not followed.** The evidence contract is still distributed rather than written as one artifact,
which Part 29 §3 specifies and does not itself perform. Three of the four new Parts break the
prevailing Part shape (§6). The instrument gating this work remains unregistered and two-valued.
The registries are extracted mechanically and **that extractor is not committed**, because the
compendium charter forbids executable code in dataset artifacts — so the extracts can drift from
the Parts and nothing will detect it. That last one is a real gap and it is the one this family's
own Part XXIII §4 would fix first: an unregistered check is layer 5 doctrine.

**Deviations, two.** Parts 27–30 are numbered in Arabic where I–XXVI are Roman, because Roman
thirty collides with a token this repository's quality gate rejects on sight; recorded in
`CLAE_INDEX.md`. And the twelfth registry was produced after the extension rather than with the
other eleven, because it reports on work that did not exist yet. Neither loss was measured, which
is the same accounting gap Part XXVI §5 admitted about its own two deviations.
