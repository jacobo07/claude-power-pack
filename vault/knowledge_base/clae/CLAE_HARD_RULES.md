# CLAE — Hard Rules Registry

> **What this file is.** Part XXVI §5 defines the twelve companion artifacts as *"extracts for retrieval convenience"* — the schemas, consolidations and measured counts live in the Parts. This file locates entries; it does not restate, resolve or extend them.
> **Reading rule.** Every row cites the Part that seeded it. Where a row's source is ambiguous, the ambiguity is transcribed rather than resolved.

## 1. The register is empty, and that is the finding

| Quantity | Count |
|---|---|
| Hard rules seeded across all 30 Parts | **0** |

Measured by the same extraction that produced the 174 process rules: zero seeds match the hard-rule form in any Part. Part XXIII §1 states the same number and gives the reason:

> **Zero hard rules is deliberate.** Part I §6 and Part II §11 placed prohibitions and invariants explicitly out of scope. *"A family about how to measure and report should not be issuing prohibitions."*

An empty register with a stated reason is a populated register. It is not the same artifact as a missing one, and the distinction is the whole point of recording it.

## 2. The promotion test

Part XXIII §2, verbatim in substance: **a process rule becomes a hard rule when its violation produces an unrecoverable consequence — not when it is violated frequently.** Promotion on frequency produces an archive of things that are merely annoying, diluting the entries that genuinely stop work.

## 3. Promotion candidates

Part XXIII §2 identifies the candidate set as *exactly* Part XXII §5's prevention-only traps — those whose violation destroys evidence that cannot be reconstructed.

| Candidate behaviour | Why violation is unrecoverable |
|---|---|
| Record provenance | Without it nothing distinguishes a captured reference from a legitimate one |
| Record intent at origin | Reconstruction is performed against the drifted artifact |
| Assign residual identity once | A re-derived identity cannot be reattached to its prior series |
| Widen instrument output | Clean results from a two-valued instrument cannot be retroactively separated into observed-nothing and did-not-run |

Part XXIII §2 is explicit about who decides: *"Whether they warrant promotion is a decision belonging to the archive's owner, not to this family."* This registry therefore records four candidates and promotes none. **Promotion is an Owner action.**
