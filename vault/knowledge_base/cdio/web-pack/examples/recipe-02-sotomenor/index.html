Four rooms under the chestnuts

A small stone guesthouse at the end of a track, in a valley that turns gold in October. Open from March to November.

Ask about dates

The house

Each room looks a different way down the valley. Thick walls keep them cool in August and warm in November.

The valley does the rest

There is a path from the door to the river, an hour down and a little more back up. In autumn the chestnut harvest fills the lanes. We leave a map in every room and keep the evenings quiet.
