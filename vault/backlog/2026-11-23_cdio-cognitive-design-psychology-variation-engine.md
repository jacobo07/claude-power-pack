## North Star

When the Owner asks for a restaurant, kitchen-renovation, law, healthcare, education or SaaS website, CPP should automatically:
1. Discover relevant, lawfully inspectable cross-sector references and the project's real business/brand truth.
2. Decompose the observable design into visual mechanisms, not just screenshots/styles.
3. Retrieve applicable evidence from cognitive science and UX research; explain plausible pathways with explicit uncertainty and boundary conditions.
4. State falsifiable audience/task predictions before selecting or optimizing variants.
5. Generate several meaningfully distinct **original** compositions by transforming and combining abstracted mechanisms, preserving brand, accessible interaction, performance, rights and factual content.
6. Evaluate actual rendered outputs and product functionality, independent human/multimodal preference where justified, and real-world controlled outcomes only when appropriate traffic, instrumentation and consent exist.
7. Improve within bounded cost, leave unresolved claims as hypotheses, and promote only repeated, transferable patterns through the current Constitutive Baseline Ratchet.

## 1. Epistemic discipline — psychology is a research program, not an aesthetic oracle

Do not claim a palette, serif face, photography style, 60/30/10 split, large heading, animation or "premium design" causes increased conversions because a demo looks good. Never equate saliency estimate with actual gaze, gaze with comprehension, comprehension with trust, stated preference with observed behavior, or simulated agents/model scores with real visitors. Never state that a person's ethnicity, age, disability or demographic group has fixed aesthetic preferences. Context and accessibility vary.

For every proposed mechanism retain typed fields:
- **Stimulus/observed feature:** actual pixel/DOM/media evidence, render viewport and source/rights.
- **Task + audience + context:** business type, user job, constraints, device, price/risk, accessibility and approved brand.
- **Candidate mechanism:** perceived organization/processing fluency, novelty/familiarity balance, contrast/salience, recognition/semantic congruence, information scent, cognitive load, genuine trust evidence, sensory imagery, distinctiveness, action clarity, motion feedback.
- **Research provenance:** primary paper or recognized standard, claim scope, study design, replicability and limitations; source-author claims are weaker than independently measured evidence.
- **Prediction:** directional, falsifiable, outcome type and predicted failure/counterexample.
- **Intervention/variant:** exactly which design factors change and which content/function/brand factors are held constant; interaction terms when multiple axes vary.
- **Outcome:** objective measurements, blinded user ratings, lab task tests, A/B results or NO_DATA; sample and uncertainty.
- **Epistemic state:** OBSERVED_VISUAL, SCIENCE_BACKED_GENERAL, PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS, RENDER_TESTED, HUMAN_PREF_TESTED, FIELD_EXPERIMENT_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED or RETIRED. More evidence needed before claiming causal transfer.
- **When NOT to use:** required, with explicit population/task/site counterexamples.
- **Risk:** autonomy, manipulative persuasion, dark patterns, inaccessible effects, privacy or mistaken authenticity.

No invented numerical psychology coefficient or pseudo-neuroscience. A fixed score from hand-authored aesthetics is a review heuristic, never causal ground truth.

### Verified starting scientific evidence (review date 2026-11-23; evaluate exact claims and newer replications at implementation)

1. Kurosu & Kashimura, CHI 1995 — *Apparent Usability vs. Inherent Usability*, https://chi1995.acm.org/proceedings/shortppr/mk_bdy.htm . Historical correlation between aesthetic properties and apparent usability; not proof that prettier = objectively more usable.
2. Reber, Schwarz & Winkielman, Psychological Science review (2004), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15582859/ . Processing fluency theory and conditions; theoretical account, not a universal conversion equation.
3. Lindgaard et al., Behaviour & Information Technology 25(2), https://doi.org/10.1080/01449290500330448 . Rapid visual appeal evaluations at brief exposure; does not prove final purchasing decisions.
4. Tuch et al., International Journal of Human–Computer Studies 70(11), https://research.google/pubs/the-role-of-visual-complexity-and-prototypicality-regarding-first-impression-of-websites-working-towards-understanding-aesthetic-judgments/ . Visual complexity and prototypicality affect first-impression ratings; culture, stimulus and context limits.
5. Liu & Gajos, CHI 2013, https://doi.org/10.1145/2470654.2481281 . Quantification of complexity/colorfulness and rapid aesthetic judgments; prediction of rated visual appeal is not conversion or individual neuropsychological inference.
6. For studies using eye tracking: attention measures are indirect and require additional performance evidence. Avoid speculative inferences from fixation alone; audit actual source/measurement limitations before adoption.

Treat this list as a **starting evidence corpus**, not an exhaustive or perpetual psychology canon. Investigate peer-reviewed challenges, population/medium differences, historical revisions and negative findings before institutional promotion.

## 2. Original design variation grammar

The generator should operate on **composable design mechanisms**, not copied project pixels. Initial variables to inspect and type using existing CCF/Visual DNA/DESIGN.md contracts:
- Narrative/focal object: verified chef/food, installed kitchen, materials, founder, actual product UI, customer journey.
- Visual hierarchy: subject placement, headline scale, CTA prominence, information priority, section rhythm.
- Palette role: neutral/base, readable text, accent use, semantic states, brand-preserving alternatives; 60/30/10 only a hypothesis.
- Typography: type personality, display/body contrast, text density, locale coverage, reading hierarchy.
- Image/texture: source role, licensed/authentic imagery, crop and material cues, subject/background relationship.
- Spatial composition: editorial, grid, asymmetric, image-led, type-led, quiet, dense, with viewport-specific constraints.
- Proof and persuasion: real testimonial/award/project, trust signal and transparent promise; no fabricated scarcity, reviews or social proof.
- Interaction: informative reveal, selection configurator, enquiry path, appointment CTA, user-feedback, adaptive mobile states and optional motion.

**Generation modes:** MIX/RECOMBINE mechanisms from different sources; MUTATE one axis at a time for identifiable effects; EXPLORE distinct art directions; REFINE selected candidate; ABSTAIN where brief/rights/source data is missing. Preserve user-approved identity and factual product constraints.

Produce **3–5 materially distinct candidate directions** when benefit/cost warrants, not trivial recolors. Keep diverse candidates and test for template collapse or excessive similarity. Do not blindly maximize novelty: recognizable sector semantics, usability and task clarity constrain originality.

**Optimization objective:** Multi-objective Pareto tradeoff, not one aesthetic number. Non-tradeable floors: accessibility, functional correctness, privacy, security, brand and asset rights, real business claims. Other measurable dimensions: comprehension, task completion, perceived appeal, recall (when measured), specific brand fit, load, page performance, conversion (only with suitable real trial) and resource cost. Record uncertainty and conflicting evaluator judgments; do not optimize exclusively on self-grading model opinions (Goodhart risk).

## 3. Experimental design — know *why* before claiming effect

A screenshot shows correlations among typography/photo/color/layout. It cannot isolate causality. For each meaningful mechanism choose:
- **Observational diagnostic:** render + human/LLM critique with evidence; yields hypotheses, NOT effectiveness claims.
- **Controlled preference test:** counterbalanced, blinded choice, suitable participants and content held consistent; measures preference/recognition, not conversion.
- **Task/usability study:** actual performance measures such as time, navigation errors, comprehension, completion and accessibility.
- **Factorial/ablation:** change one variable or plan orthogonal factors; log interactions and avoid attributing multiple simultaneous changes to one cause.
- **Online A/B testing:** only if traffic, consent/privacy, allocation/randomization, power, metric definitions and guardrails justify it. No low-sample winner declarations; maintain control group and non-manipulative consent-aware design.

Candidate algorithmic frameworks requiring **selective** technical/rights/ROI evaluation rather than default installation:
- [Optuna](https://github.com/optuna/optuna): Python search over varied design parameter configurations if objective observations are available.
- [Ax](https://github.com/meta/Ax): constrained, multi-objective Bayesian/adaptive experimentation when evaluations are expensive and dataset/evidence justify the complexity.
- [pymoo](https://github.com/anyoptimization/pymoo): multi-objective evolutionary search/Pareto candidate sets for a genuinely large design search space.
- [GrowthBook](https://github.com/growthbook/growthbook): real production feature flags/experiment analysis if customer traffic and organizational consent/data access warrant it; open-core rights review.
- Existing Playwright + CDIO rendered checks: source of **actual observations**, not a psychological oracle.
- Existing CCF, CDICF and design index: current owners for rendering inputs, identity, asset rights and patterns.

No autonomous production A/B deployments, behavioral data collection or paid experiments without appropriate authorization.
