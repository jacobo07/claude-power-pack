### Required autonomous processing ladder

The mission must produce and prove these separate stages. Do not collapse them into one model summary or claim complete absorption from a JSONL import.

1. **Discover:** bounded and permitted acquisition of source directories, links and owner-authorized collections, with provenance/identity, dedupe, freshness and error telemetry.
2. **Observe:** inspect accessible public material, making separate claims for actually visible information, source-author claims, inferences and unknowns; no claims of source-code inspection from screenshots.
3. **Distill:** record transferable chromatic, typographic, photograph, composition, material, motion, interaction, brand, conversion and responsive grammars; preserve counterexamples and a concrete "WHEN NOT TO USE".
4. **Classify:** map each case along independent axes of industry/subindustry, audience/job-to-be-done, surface type, brand style, visual mechanism, media/source availability, accessibility, risk and degree of proof; support cross-sector transfer rather than sector-specific cliché.
5. **Select:** retrieve only relevant options for a user task, justify at least one rejected comparator, preserve approved business/brand constraints and honor legal/use restrictions. Abstention is valid.
6. **Construct:** build original solutions or legally cleared component/asset derivatives through CCF, design-md, CDICF and existing frontend runtime. A reference never becomes a deployable kit by renaming a screenshot.
7. **Render & critique:** browser/device/state captures, actual functional flows, accessibility and performance, independently assessed subjective brand/visual quality with observed evidence; diagnose and repair material defects.
8. **Promote:** only after positive/negative tests and cross-project proof, publish an abstracted reproducible pattern or approved deployable kit and connect its consumer to existing CDIO retrieval and Knowledge Vault/UKDL.
9. **Refresh or retire:** detect stale/broken URLs, changed terms and retired patterns, invalidate unsafe caches and stop suggesting obsolete assets.

## Distinguish four different library objects

1. **SOURCE:** Directory/source API or gallery; not itself a design kit.
2. **REFERENCE:** A designer's published case study; can inspire analysis but grants no reuse license.
3. **PATTERN:** Original abstracted and independently reproducible recipe with source provenance, applicability, when-not-to-use, and original fixtures.
4. **DEPLOYABLE KIT:** Approved original/cleared assets plus coherent DESIGN.md-compatible design system, components, implementation and rendered evidence, with clear rights and licensing.

Never promote SOURCE/REFERENCE directly to DEPLOYABLE KIT. A URL is not proof of access to a source asset, and a screenshot is not proof of an operational website.
