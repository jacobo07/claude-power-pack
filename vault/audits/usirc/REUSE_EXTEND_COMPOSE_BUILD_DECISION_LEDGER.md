## Sizing check on the survivors

Under the estate's own novelty bar (HR-NOVELTY-001), a family needs multiple
mechanisms, multiple consumers and its own lifecycle. The three survivors have,
between them, **three mechanisms, four consumers and one shared lifecycle** — and two
of the three are instruments that CLAE Part XIII already has a taxonomy slot for.

That is a **module with a schema and two instruments**, on the order of 1,500–2,500
lines of Python plus one dataset's worth of doctrine if the Owner wants the doctrine
written. It is not thirteen categories and it is not 160 datasets.
