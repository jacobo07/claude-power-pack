(about "digital twin") and then violates it six more times.

## Standing rule this report proposes

A new artifact may not adopt a noun already sealed by another family without a
qualifier that makes the two readable side by side in one sentence. The estate has 26
dataset families; nouns are scarce and collisions are silent until two agents answer
the same question differently.
