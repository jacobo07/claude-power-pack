## Result

**0 of 30 candidates clear the 13-question novelty gate as `GENUINELY_NEW_DATASET`.**
This is the sixth consecutive mega-corpus proposal for this repo (AISHF, RE
Baseline's 3 NEW families, KSF's 22, the UKR Compendium's 8-step escalation,
and now these 30) to measure as majority-or-fully owned once checked against a
discovered denominator instead of the corpus's own curated list.

**AD (Institutional Novelty Proof System)** is the one candidate with a real,
named gap: nothing in this repo *automatically* runs the novelty check before
a new fabric/OS/platform/dataset is proposed — it has only ever happened
because an Owner manually invoked it, five times, at real cost each time. But
AD itself does not clear its own bar as an independent dataset (it has one
mechanism, one consumer, one lifecycle) — it is a **Hard Rule + a ~40-line
extension to `modules/spec_gate/gate.py`**, not a family with Parts, engines,
registries, and a charter.

## Recommendation (STOP #1)

Do not construct any of D1–D12. Do not open new dataset families. The only
recommended action: codify **PR-NOVELTY-PROOF-REQUIRED-001** as a Hard Rule
(HR-tier, sealed in this repo's `CLAUDE.md`) and wire its 13-question test
into `spec_gate.check_spec_gate()` so any future "N-family fabric" proposal is
caught at the spec stage — before a multi-thousand-line audit is needed to
discover what could have been a single gate check.
