# GSDX-MIL: Mission Intelligence Loop (successor of GSDX-CI, plan 2026-11-09)

## Context
GSDX-CI closed PARTIAL (9/9 phases, gates green). The capabilities exist, but measured benefit is thin. The
biggest measured loss was neither a missing intelligence nor a missing capability. It was **the mission
machinery itself**:
- 8.7M tokens went into a planner subagent that produced nothing.
- About 5 h passed with nobody working, in two worker blocks.
- One directive was never delivered.
- The brief was never stored.

The Owner asks to "take it much further". The leverage is to make GSD X **learn from its own missions,
automatically, with measured savings**. That puts the 15 capabilities on the one dataset that is large, real and
already ours, instead of on synthetic cases:
- GEX44 ledger: 110 missions, 10,944 events (299 breaker trips, 76 blocks, 25 escalations, 250 refused markers,
  67 unfunded closeouts, 93 in-place compactions, 19 economic incidents).
- Laptop: 663 mission records.

North star:
1. A closed mission is mined.
2. Its waste is classified by cause.
3. The class with the highest measured cost gets a guard.
4. The guard is replayed on history to predict what it would have saved.
5. It is canaried.
6. The next missions measure the real saving.
7. The learning lands as a recall lesson with an invalidator, and the next mission inherits it.

That loop is the brief's Causal, Counterfactual, Scientific, Capital, Knowledge Revision, Competence and Ratchet
capabilities, all working on one real loop.

## Phases (one Ralph mission on GEX44; inline plans, no Agent-tool ceremony)
- **P0 Reconcile and freeze.**
  - Brief (this plan plus the Owner's original brief) stored verbatim with its hash.
  - Freeze the ledger corpora: GEX44 plus the laptop copy, sha256. Split by time into train
    (≤ 2026-11-01) and holdout (after).
  - `tools/test_gsdx_mil.py --wave p0`.
- **P1 Waste miner (deterministic).** `tools/mission_waste.py`.
  - Classes:
    - tokens with no product commit;
    - idle while BLOCKED with authority remaining (a false escalation);
    - directive recorded but never delivered;
    - subagent negative EVC;
    - relay held;
    - closeout unfunded loops;
    - marker refused.
  - For every class, measure tokens and hours lost per mission.
  - Pre-registered: the predicted top 3 classes, committed before the first run.
  - Mutants: synthetic missions with known waste must be found; clean ones must not.
- **P2 Guards for the top classes**, each a small extension of an existing owner, with its predicted saving
  replayed on the frozen train corpus and then judged on the holdout:
  - **G1** `arm` requires a `--work-unit` / packet verdict. When the gate says DIRECT/DELTA, the card forbids
    planner subagents (`planner_agent_admitted=false`).
  - **G2** the rehydration card and the wall message state **remaining authority**. A worker block that claims
    capital exhaustion while authority remains is refused and auto-answered (D1 pattern, no Owner wait).
  - **G3** directives are also delivered at `SessionStart source=compact` (the in-place autocompact case).
  - **G4** `arm` refuses a packet with no verbatim brief and hash.
  - **G5** whatever P1 ranks above these that is not on this list. The data decides, not this plan.
- **P3 Make GSDX-CI capabilities effective.**
  - Backfill `invalidated_when` on the 15 CME METHOD-OUTCOME lessons, taken from their `reconsider_when`.
  - Give `evaluate_transition` a real caller, or retire it.
  - Feed the computed blast radius into the G1 verdict.
  - Each item gets a mutant.
- **P4 Automatic Error→Immunity.**
  - On every closed mission, the sweep runs the miner and writes trap-candidate lessons with
    `invalidated_when` and provenance (mission id, ledger seq).
  - The UKDL itself is not touched automatically: candidates only, promotion stays human.
  - Fresh-agent test: a new mission inherits a mined lesson, ≥5 tasks.
- **P5 Transfer experiment done right.**
  - The Lab C corpus is chosen by a pilot (control < 0.7), then n ≥ 30 per arm with lean `claude -p` flags,
    haiku.
  - Cap: 150 calls, 0.5M child tokens.
- **P6 Canary and prospective measurement (Owner gate).**
  - Ask the Owner, with the gate output, to deploy G1-G4 to the GEX44 live clone behind kill switches
    (`CPP_MIL_*=off`).
  - The next K≥5 real missions are measured by the miner against the P1 baseline: tokens per product commit,
    idle hours, Owner interventions.
  - The mission parks with a CHECKPOINT and the measurement is collected when those missions close. It is never
    claimed before then.
- **P7 Adversarial tests, meta-analysis (brief XLII, 33 questions), UKDL three levels, handoff.**
