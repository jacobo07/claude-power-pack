## 2. The finding that shapes everything

The source corpus **agrees with the estate's duplication discipline.** At lines
30,124–30,186 it makes an explicit architectural decision *not* to build USIFB as its own
mega-silo with its own databases, graphs and detectors, but as **analysis modes and
discovery strategies layered over six shared institutional planes**. The inventory agent
recorded this as a recurring pattern: "a persistent architectural anxiety about
proliferating mega-silos, repeatedly resolved by declaring new systems as analysis modes
over shared primitives rather than new independent infrastructure."

This is the same shape as the CRPF strike, where the donor corpus's own D2A map had
already scored its mechanisms as EXTEND. **A build that spawns 60+ sovereign fabrics would
be contradicting the source, not honouring it.**

Simultaneously, §3 of the mission brief resolves the tension and is adopted verbatim:

> **DATASET IDENTITY ≠ RUNTIME PROCESS BOUNDARY.**

Every named system receives dataset-level coverage. The D2A verdict decides the *form*
that coverage takes, never whether it exists.

## 9. Risks

1. **Redundant corpus** — the estate's dominant historical failure mode. Mitigated by
   Phase 3 evidence-cited verdicts and the Phase 4 STOP.
2. **Depth dilution** — many Parts, shallow each. Mitigated by the inherited per-Part floor
   applied *per Part*, not per dataset.
3. **Scale** — a maximal reading of §9 implies 1,200–1,600 Parts (~1.8 M words), which would
   roughly double the entire estate. This is the Owner decision at the head of §10.
4. **Context loss** — mitigated by durable campaign state and file-based agent handoff.
5. **False novelty from vocabulary zeros** — mitigated by mandatory capability-level
   re-sweep before any CREATE verdict.

## 10. The one decision that must be made before Phase 6

§9 ("a full multi-Part dataset per system") and §3/§8 ("minimum number of canonical owners")
admit two consistent readings, and they differ by roughly an order of magnitude in cost.
Both satisfy §5 zero-omission. The Owner picks; this plan executes either.

- **A — D2A-gated corpus.** Sovereign multi-Part datasets only where Phase 3 measures a
  real gap (base rate suggests ~8–15). Every other named system receives a full
  Systems-Derived-Catalog entry: identity, aliases, definition, responsibilities,
  contracts, integration, failure modes, and the declared canonical owner with the reason
  it is not independent. Matches the estate's sealed discipline and the source's own ruling.
- **B — Maximal corpus.** A full ~20-Part dataset for every system-KIND concept
  (~60–80 datasets, 1,200–1,600 Parts). Literal reading of §9. Accepts heavy overlap with
  `cpp_ias`, `d2a_fabric`, `clae` and `crawl_os`, against a measured 9–36 % historical
  CREATE rate.
- **C — Tiered.** Full sovereign datasets for measured gaps; **mid-depth datasets
  (6–10 Parts)** for spine systems that are EXTEND-verdict but carry a distinct
  institutional mission; catalog entries for the remainder.
