## Two founding premises are false

### G9's retirement field does not exist

The gap table states: *"Hard rules carry a retirement condition field. No process ever
evaluates it."* The `Rule` dataclass (`modules/rule_compiler/schema.py:111`) carries
`rule_id · title · form · source · trigger · stop · evidence · exception · severity · body ·
rejections`. **There is no retirement condition field.** The complaint is vacuous: no
process evaluates it because there is nothing to evaluate. Building an engine to read a
field that was never recorded would have been the whole family's most load-bearing Part.

The real, smaller question G9 should have asked: a rule records `evidence` — the incident
that produced it — so retirement means *this incident class can no longer occur*. Nothing
records that, and no field change is required to start.

### M4 is already risk-weighted

The premise is *risk-weighted, not count-weighted*. The live predicate is **`CRITICAL` OR
`recurrence >= 3`** — a CRITICAL bug promotes at first occurrence, which is risk-weighting.
The genuine residual is graded rather than binary risk, in one function.

## G8: the mechanism works, and has been ineffective for 66 days

This is the audit's most consequential measurement, and it argues *against* building rather
than for it.

**The detector fires.** `vault/handoffs/` holds **333 handoffs, 332 of them mirror-drift.**

**All 332 are the same pair.** Every one names
`~/.claude/knowledge_vault/core/apex-completion-standard.md` against its PP mirror. Oldest
**2026-05-23**, newest **2026-07-27 19:03**. The delta has *grown* to **2,083 lines /
117,206 bytes**.

A detector that has raised the identical alert 332 times across 66 days while the condition
worsened is not providing detection. It is producing noise that trains its reader to ignore
it — and no part of the estate notices, because nothing measures whether a governance
surface achieves anything.

**That is G7's question, answered empirically by G8's own output.** The missing capability
is not a fabric. It is one property: **a detector must escalate when its own alert goes
unresolved.** Repeat-alert-without-resolution is the measurable signal, and it is already
sitting on disk, counted.

## Recommendation

Not a decision. The decision is the Owner's.

- **A — Escalation on unresolved repeat.** The 332/66-day case is live evidence and its own
  first test. Smallest unit, highest measured value, closes G7 and G8's real defect at once.
- **B — Discovery producer for mirror pairs.** Replace two hand-written lists with a scan of
  both hook directories, line-ending-normalised. Directly measurable: 5 watched → 68 visible.
- **C — Resolve the two-month drift itself.** 2,083 lines apart. Owner-side, because
  `~/.claude/knowledge_vault/` is global config (HR-001).
- **D — Build IGEF as chartered.** Available, and it would author twenty Parts over three
  mechanisms that are owned and one premise that is false. Recorded, not recommended.

Sequencing precedent: CRPF ran repair-first (Option A), then shipped the genuine residue
inside the family that already owned the physics (Option B'). The same shape fits here:
the residue belongs to `rule_compiler` and the mirror verifier, not to a new fabric.
