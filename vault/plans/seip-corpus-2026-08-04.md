## The one genuine residue — G3 / Ley XV

**`baseline_guardian.py` is asymmetric by construction.** Opened this session: it exists
to ensure *"protection may not be withdrawn in silence"* — it detects and names a
**loss**. `run_sqi.py` exits non-zero on a silent decrease. The entire quality substrate
is built to make the bar impossible to lower.

**Nothing in the estate raises the bar.** An estate-wide sweep of `modules/*/*.py` and
`tools/*.py` for difficulty escalation returns exactly one file, and it is
`unknown_unknown_generator.py`, a different object with a different output. There is no
saturation detector, no difficulty-escalation proposer, and no consumer for the
sentence the Owner made the centrepiece of this brief:

> when a benchmark reaches 100 % it is not perfect — it means the bar must be raised.

This is **Ley XV** — the one law of the fifteen with no enforcement mechanism — and it is
the one thing the source contributes that this estate demonstrably does not own. It is
also the one place where the KobiMapEngine philosophy is genuinely additive rather than
a rename.

**Shape of the real fix**, sized honestly: a saturation predicate over SQI's existing
baseline (stable performance across independent runs, holdout survival, no fixture
dependence, no regression in sibling dimensions — the source's own eight-condition
Saturation Gate is a good specification), plus a proposer that emits difficulty
candidates along the existing `BenchmarkScenario` axes, plus the counterfactual probe
kind for F4. One module, one registry extension, one V-gate. **Propose-only** — a gate
that raises its own bar autonomously is a gate that grades itself, which is the exact
defect `sqi_scs_c93` was sealed to prevent.

That is on the order of 600–900 lines of Python and one registry file. It is not
thirteen families, and it is not 56 datasets.

## The architectural decision the brief asked for

The brief asked whether SEIP and USSC are levels of one system (A), whether SEIP is
subordinate to USSC (B), whether USSC is the vision and SEIP the runtime (C), or
whether a better architecture exists (D).

**D — and the better architecture is that neither entity should exist as a named thing.**

Both names denote a *coordination function over existing organs*. The estate already
discharges that function through three shipped owners: `capability_runtime` (capability
contracts and applicability, shipped 2026-08-04), `graphify` (the institutional graph),
and `sqi` (the quality substrate the whole loop is scored against). Adding a control
plane above them creates an org chart, not a mechanism — which is precisely what
`T-IIC-ORGCHART-IS-NOT-MECHANISM-001` exists to refuse. The source's §13 argues this
case against everyone else's proposals and then exempts its own.

The purpose the brief cares about is preserved without either name: the loop it
describes already runs, organ by organ, and its single missing link is the ratchet — the
thing that makes generation *n+1* start from a strictly harder bar than generation *n*.
Build the ratchet. Do not build the civilization around it.
