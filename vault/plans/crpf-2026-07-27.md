## PASO −1 — The criterion behind "3 NEW + 5 EXTEND"

The Owner required this extracted before scope is fixed, per CLAE Part XVII §10 (an answer is
evidence about what was shown; reusing a verdict without its criterion buys the same question
twice).

### Finding 1 — the criterion was never written down. `VERIFIED`

`vault/plans/re-baseline-compendium-2026-07-26.md` records verdicts (REUSE / REUSE+EXTEND /
EXTEND / GENERALIZE / DO-NOT-BUILD) and a gap table (G1–G10). It contains no statement of the
rule that assigns them. The charter inherits the verdicts and likewise states no rule. The
criterion is therefore reconstructed from the verdict pattern, and everything below it is
labelled `INFERRED` unless separately verified.

### Finding 2 — two different evidence standards ran under one verdict label. `VERIFIED`

- **CLAE (G1–G5) was admitted by measured absence.** The audit states: a repo-wide
  case-insensitive search for the mechanism vocabulary (`reference-delta`, `quality distance`,
  `anti-underbuild`, `human oracle`, `observability-capable`, `phase zero`, `deviation ledger`)
  returned **zero files**. Absence was an observation.
- **CRPF (G6) was admitted by asserted absence.** No zero-hit grep is recorded for G6. Its
  justification is the sentence *"nothing tiers the assets"* plus a three-entry nearest-owner
  column (`memory-engine` hot/cold · `graphify` route compiler context pack ·
  `daif_08_context_runtime`). Absence was a claim.

The reconstructed operating criterion is therefore: **NEW when a vocabulary sweep returns
zero AND no single existing owner can host the addition; EXTEND when a single owner's shape
can hold it; REFERENCE when PP has no substrate at all.** `INFERRED`

Under that criterion CLAE qualifies. **CRPF does not — its first conjunct was never tested.**

### Finding 3 — the mechanism that produced the miss is one this repo has already named. `VERIFIED`

The nearest-owner column was hand-enumerated from what the auditor recalled. `cognitive_os` —
twelve sealed datasets, eleven modules, an index, and a live-implementation table — is absent
from every row of Phase 0. It was not scored as a competing owner and found wanting; it was
never in the denominator, and its absence read as a gap.

That is `PR-COVERAGE-BY-CONSTRUCTION-001` (project `CLAUDE.md`, Liveness Standard corollary):
*an audit whose subjects are enrolled by hand measures memory, not reality.* It is the same
defect the audit itself diagnosed in `SKILLBANK.md` and `vault/knowledge_base/INDEX.md` on
page one — applied to its own nearest-owner column, in the same document.

**Consequence for the criterion, stated as a rule and not as a regret:** an ownership
denominator must be *discovered* (enumerate `modules/` and `vault/knowledge_base/` and search
the mechanism's vocabulary across all of it) before any gap is graded NEW. A three-entry
recalled list is not a denominator.
