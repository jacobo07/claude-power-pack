## 3. Iteration applied (CLASE classification of the brief's own premises)
- **CLASE 2 (state premise false):** "build in parallel with current work". CME is live in the same product
  surface (gsd_mission.py pivot code, recall lessons). Change: **this programme launches only after CME
  reaches COMPLETED**, branches from the gsdx-cme HEAD, and inherits its corpora, gates and traps.
- **CLASE 1 (instrument premise false):** "12M/24M fresh tokens". The breaker counts processed tokens, and
  CME records the worker's fresh-vs-gross split as UNKNOWN. Change: authority is expressed in breaker units,
  which are stricter than fresh (fresh is never more than processed), so the ceiling cannot be overrun
  silently.
- **CLASE 2:** "a 5-arm benchmark (A-E) across 4 labs". CME showed n=7 with no positives and a
  non-discriminating control. Five arms over four labs on 24M would produce noise. Change: one pre-registered
  falsifier per slice, plus a **single A-vs-E comparison** on the one corpus with enough n (RF frozen decisions
  plus a new positive holdout). Ablation is per slice: remove the slice and the named decision must change.
- **Work elimination:** 9 of the 15 get CERTIFY: a run of their real CLI on a real input plus one faithful
  mutant (deterministic, about 0 model tokens). Only 6 slices change code.
- **Overengineering rejected:**
  - a new IR, decision DB, metacognition layer, scheduler, or agent topology;
  - copying RF modules into PP. A second domain uses the RF pattern only through S5's probe.
